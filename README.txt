NARRATOR - listen to your ebooks
================================

Narrator reads EPUB, PDF, Word (.docx) and text books aloud with natural-sounding
AI voices. Everything runs on your own computer: no account, no internet needed
after setup, nothing is sent anywhere.

SETUP (once)
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\Narrator.
  2. Double-click Setup.bat. It installs what Narrator needs (about 3 GB) and
     puts a Narrator shortcut on your desktop. Takes 10-20 minutes.
     If Python isn't installed it will try to install it for you.

  Works on Windows 10/11. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it much faster but
  isn't required.

USING IT
  - Open Narrator from the desktop shortcut (or Start Narrator.bat).
  - Add books: drag them into the window, drop them on the desktop icon,
    or click "Add books".
  - Click a book to continue where you left off. Click any paragraph to start
    reading from there.
  - Player buttons: previous/next chapter, back/forward 15 seconds,
    previous/next paragraph, speed, voice (28 voices with samples),
    sleep timer, bookmarks, volume (up to 160%).
  - "Make audiobook file" saves the book as an .m4b (one file with chapters)
    or MP3s, for your phone or car.
  - Press ? in the app for keyboard shortcuts.

  Narrator remembers your place in every book, your voice, speed and volume.

NOTES
  - Books with copy protection (DRM), like most Kindle purchases, can't be read.
  - Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) have no text to read.
  - Your library and reading positions are stored in state.json in this folder.

Voice engine: Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0).
